# 參考資料

* [Programming Language Foundations in Agda](https://plfa.github.io/) -- 作者：Philip Wadler、Wen Kokke 與 Jeremy Siek
* [Programming and Proving in Agda](https://github.com/jespercockx/agda-lecture-notes/raw/master/agda.pdf) -- 作者：Jesper Cockx
* [Introduction to Univalent Foundations of Mathematics with Agda](https://www.cs.bham.ac.uk/~mhe/HoTT-UF-in-Agda-Lecture-Notes/index.html) -- 作者：Martín Escardó
* [Dependent Types at Work](http://www.cse.chalmers.se/~peterd/papers/DependentTypesAtWork.pdf) -- 作者：Ana Bove 與 Peter Dybjer
* [Learn you an Agda](https://williamdemeo.github.io/2014/02/27/learn-you-an-agda/) -- 作者：Liam O'Connor-Davis，William DeMeo 補充
* [A practical Agda tutorial](https://people.inf.elte.hu/divip/AgdaTutorial/Index.html) -- 作者：Péter Diviánszky 與 Ambrus Kaposi
* [Correct-by-construction programming in Agda](https://jespercockx.github.io/ohrid19-agda/) -- 作者：Jesper Cockx
* [More on the double-negation translation](https://www.speicherleck.de/iblech/stuff/slides-fischbachau2022.pdf)
* [Interactive Theorem Proving](https://p8.cs.fau.de/itp/) -- 作者：Thorsten Wißmann
* [1Lab](https://1lab.dev/) -- 作者：Amélia Liao 與其他協作者
* [TypeTopology](https://martinescardo.github.io/TypeTopology/) -- 作者：Martín Escardó 與其他協作者
